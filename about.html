Our story

About Urban Nuts

A nationwide supplier of premium nuts, dried fruit, seeds and artisan coated snacks — rooted in Cape Town.

Specialty food, without the clutter

Urban Nuts was established to bring premium quality nuts, dried fruit, health products, superfoods and artisan coated snacks to South African homes and businesses. We operate retail stores across Cape Town and supply customers nationwide online.

This preview reimagines the shopping experience: calm black-and-white branding, clear pricing in Rand, and a cart that makes free delivery over R1 000 impossible to miss.

What we stand for

Freshness, fair pricing, and service — whether you collect from Access Park Kenilworth or order to your door in Johannesburg. Resellers are welcome; ask us about wholesale.

Visit or order

Find us at Access Park Kenilworth, Edgemead Village, N1 City, Boulevard Square Brackenfell, Haasendal Gables and Eikestad Mall Stellenbosch — or shop this demo catalogue online.
